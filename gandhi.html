Mahatma Gandhi ji (1869 - 1948)

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi ji, known as Mahatma Gandhi, was born on October 2, 1869 in Porbandar, Gujarat. He is remembered as the Father of the Nation for his pivotal role in India’s independence struggle through the principles of truth (Satya) and non-violence (Ahimsa).

Gandhi ji led major movements such as the Champaran Satyagraha, the Non-Cooperation Movement, the Salt March (Dandi March), and the Quit India Movement, which shook the foundations of British rule. His simple lifestyle, moral courage, and philosophy of peace continue to inspire people across the world.

Key Facts

Born September 28, 1907 (Banga, Punjab)
Known For Revolutionary activities against British rule
Famous Slogan "Inquilab Zindabad" (Long Live the Revolution)
Martyrdom March 23, 1931 (Shaheed Diwas)
