Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel ji (1875 - 1950)

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel ji, popularly known as the "Iron Man of India", was one of the most prominent leaders of the freedom movement and played a crucial role in the integration of independent India. Born on October 31, 1875, in Nadiad, Gujarat, Patel ji was a strong follower of Mahatma Gandhi ji and actively participated in movements like the Non-Cooperation Movement and the Quit India Movement.

After independence, as India’s first Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister, he united more than 500 princely states into the Indian Union, earning him the title "Bismarck of India".

Key Facts

Born October 31, 1875 (Nadiad, Gujarat)
Known For Unification of princely states, Iron Man of India
Famous Title "Bismarck of India"
Died December 15, 1950 (Bombay, now Mumbai)

Sardar Patel’s leadership and determination united India into one nation.
