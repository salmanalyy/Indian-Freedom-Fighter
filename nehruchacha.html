Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru ji (1889 - 1964)

Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru ji was one of the foremost leaders of India’s freedom struggle and later became the first Prime Minister of independent India. Born on November 14, 1889, in Allahabad, he was a close associate of Mahatma Gandhi ji and actively participated in movements like the Non-Cooperation Movement and Quit India Movement. Nehru ji envisioned India as a modern, scientific, and industrial nation. He was deeply loved by children, who fondly called him “Chacha Nehru,” and his birthday is celebrated as Children’s Day in India.

Key Facts

Born November 14, 1889 (Allahabad, Uttar Pradesh)
Known For First Prime Minister of India, visionary leader
Famous Slogan “Tryst with Destiny” (Independence speech, 1947)
Died May 27, 1964 (New Delhi)

Pandit Nehru’s vision laid the foundation of modern India.
