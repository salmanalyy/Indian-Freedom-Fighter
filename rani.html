Shaheed Bhagat Singh (1907 - 1931 )

Bhagat Singh was one of the most courageous and influential revolutionaries in India's struggle for independence. Born on September 28, 1907, in Banga village of Punjab, he grew up witnessing the atrocities of British rule, which inspired him to dedicate his life to the cause of freedom.

His fearless acts against the British, such as the Central Legislative Assembly bombing in 1929 and the protest against the Simon Commission, made him a symbol of youth bravery and patriotism. Bhagat Singh was executed on March 23, 1931, along with Rajguru and Sukhdev, and is fondly remembered as "Shaheed-e-Azam" (The Great Martyr).

Key Facts

Born September 28, 1907 (Banga, Punjab)
Known For Revolutionary activities against British rule
Famous Slogan "Inquilab Zindabad" (Long Live the Revolution)
Martyrdom March 23, 1931 (Shaheed Diwas)

Bhagat Singh’s courage and sacrifice continue to inspire generations of Indians.
